# Hook lab

1. If you don't know it yet, ask once: who is this for and what do they get?
2. Write 12 hooks, 2 of each type: result first, mistake, curiosity gap, contrarian, list, "you" callout.
3. Score each one from 1 to 5 on: specific (a number, name or result), clearly for someone, the content pays it off, short (9 words spoken, 12 written). Drop anything under 16/20.
4. Give me the top 3, each with the on-screen text (5 words max) and the first spoken line.

Never: "In this video", "Hey guys", questions with an obvious answer, or a promise the content doesn't keep.
